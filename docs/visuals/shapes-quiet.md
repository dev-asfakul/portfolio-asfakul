# Quiet Shape Language

Quiet uses one large soft organic form per scene, occasionally supported by a low-contrast gradient field. Edges are blurred and transitions are broad; the shape should feel like atmosphere with a center of gravity, not an illustration. Off-white and charcoal establish the surface while muted terracotta provides a restrained warmth. Contrast stays low so content can remain the most articulate element.

Motion is near-stillness by design. The form breathes through a two-percent scale range and very slow opacity shifts over four-to-eight-second durations. Scroll movement is minimal: the shape holds its position while content passes over it, with only a restrained scene-level reveal when entering. There is no aggressive scrub, rotation, or morphing. The system should feel settled even while it is technically alive.

Quiet does not respond to the cursor. Pointer tracking, magnetic pull, tilt, hover attraction, and repulsion are all intentionally absent. This is part of the mood’s behavior, not an omitted enhancement. Reduced motion is visually identical to the default static composition: the organic form remains in place with the same blur, scale, opacity, and hierarchy, while breathing and scroll reveal are removed.

Quiet is rendered with CSS for the lightest gradient field or SVG when a single soft vector form is needed. The registry declares one dominant shape, optional field, blur treatment, palette, static position, and very long timing parameters. If the scene begins to accumulate multiple competing forms, it has left Quiet’s vocabulary and must be reduced back to one readable atmosphere.
