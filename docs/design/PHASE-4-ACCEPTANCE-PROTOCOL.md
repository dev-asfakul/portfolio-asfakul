# Phase 4 acceptance protocol

**Historical reference only — Phase 4 is CLOSED AS-IS by owner decision.** The blur and story acceptance tests below were waived; do not run them. No viewer results should be entered or inferred. The procedure is retained for audit history, not as a Phase 5 gate.

## Blur test

1. Open Act 3 (The Turn) in Quiet, then Editorial, then Play at 1440w. Screenshot each.
2. Apply a heavy Gaussian blur (≥20px) until text is unreadable.
3. Place the three blurred images side by side.
4. Show the result to 5 people unfamiliar with the project.
5. Ask: “Same design or three different designs?”
6. Record answers verbatim.

Pass: 4 of 5 say “three different”.

## 5-second test

Show the home page for 5 seconds with no scrolling. Ask: “What does this studio do? What would you do next?” Record answers verbatim.

## Mute test

Show the same home page while reading no text. Record what viewers infer from the visual system and what action they would take.

## Continuity test

Screen-record the full desktop scroll. Watch it back and ask: “Does any act feel like a separate template?” Record answers verbatim.

## Mind-change test

After the full scroll, ask each viewer: “What do you think of this designer, and would you contact him?” Record answers verbatim.

## Report format

Record the date, viewport, viewer count, exact prompts, answers verbatim, pass/fail per test, observations, and owner sign-off in `docs/evidence/phase-4-closeout.md`.

Human acceptance was owner-run only while the protocol was active. The owner has waived the tests and closed Phase 4 as-is; v0 must not fabricate viewer answers, mark tests passed, or reopen the phase.
